Small tools for the links and codes you meet every day

One page, four tabs, nothing to install.

  • 🧹Clean links

    Removes tracking parameters, unwraps redirects, and shows which app or site a link belongs to before you tap it.

  • 📷Scan codes

    Read a QR code or barcode from a photo, a dropped image or your camera, then clean and check whatever is inside.

  • 🛡️Threat check

    Gives every link a verdict, so a suspicious one gets flagged before you open it.

  • 🔳Make a code

    Turn a link into a QR code you can show on screen or save.

  • 📹Call test

    Check your camera, microphone and speakers before a meeting, so you find the problem first.

  • 🤖A helper that learns

    Ask in English or Singlish. Teach it an answer once and it remembers, on your device only.

Your links stay on your device

PeekABooCode is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. There is no backend to send anything to.

  • Cleaning, scanning and code generation all run inside your browser tab.
  • Settings and anything the helper learns are saved in local storage on your device.
  • No account, no sign-up, no analytics on what you paste.
