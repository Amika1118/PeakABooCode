§Privacy

The short version: nothing leaves your browser unless you ask it to.

What we don't do

  • No accounts
  • No cookies
  • No analytics
  • No advertising
  • No server logs
  • No fingerprinting
  • No third-party trackers

We don't collect, transmit, store, or sell anything about you. There's no backend to send it to.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in the app happens in your browser. This includes:

  • Links you clean - processed in the browser, never uploaded.
  • QR / barcode images you upload - decoded in the browser, never uploaded.
  • Camera and microphone - used only for the pre-call test. No recording, no upload. Tracks are released the moment you click Stop or switch tabs.
  • Your settings - custom apps, custom params, theme, bot learning. All stored in localStorage on your device.
  • The help bot - everything it learns stays in localStorage.

Network requests

These are the only times anything leaves your browser. All three are opt-in - none of them happen automatically.

1. Short link expansion

When you click Expand on a short link (like bit.ly or t.co), your browser sends a request to that shortener to follow its redirect. We don't proxy it, log it, or see it. If the shortener blocks browser requests (CORS), we tell you honestly and offer an Open link button instead.

2. Online threat check

When you click Yes, check in the threat banner, we send the domain to a free, keyless security API. It returns a verdict: trusted, caution, suspicious, or unknown. Nothing is stored on our end. If the API is unreachable, we say so instead of guessing.

3. Search this code

When you scan a barcode and click Search this code, we open DuckDuckGo in a new tab with the code as the query. That's the only thing that request does.

There are no other network requests from the app itself. Library files for QR generation, barcode scanning, and spreadsheet export are loaded from local assets/lib/ whenever possible, with CDN fallbacks only if a local file is missing.

Permissions

We ask for the camera and microphone only when you start the pre-call test. We explain why before the browser prompt appears. You can deny, and the rest of the app works fine.

If you allow, the camera and mic stay active only while the test is running. Switching browser tabs or site tabs releases them immediately. Coming back asks whether you want to test again.

Third-party libraries

The app uses a small number of open-source libraries:

  • qrcodejs - QR generation (MIT)
  • JsBarcode - barcode generation (MIT)
  • ZXing - QR / barcode decoding (Apache 2.0)
  • SheetJS - spreadsheet export (Apache 2.0)

None of them phone home. They run entirely in your browser.

Your data, your control

You can see everything the app stores:

  • Open Settings to view and edit custom apps and custom tracking params.
  • Use Export to download your settings as JSON, CSV, XLS, or a printed PDF.
  • Use Reset to clear custom entries. Built-in defaults stay.
  • To fully clear everything, open your browser's site settings and clear storage for this site.

Terms

PeekABooCode is a convenience tool, not a security guarantee or an emergency service. We do our best to flag suspicious links, but no tool catches everything. Always use your own judgment before clicking, entering passwords, or entering payment info.
